## Method

- **Audience sizes:** YouTube subscriber counts and last uploads come from each channel's /about and /videos pages. Newsletter, podcast and blog figures are each creator's own claims on their own site, which are not audited.
- **Contacts:** only methods the creator lists publicly for business, collaboration or advertising. "YT About button" means YouTube's "View email address", which sits behind a CAPTCHA and was not opened. Before an email is listed here, its domain was fetched and returned HTTP 200. Where a site hides its address behind spam protection, only the page is listed and the address was not decoded.
- **What was not done:** no one was contacted. No forms, logins, contact databases or email-format guessing were used.
- **Activity:** "INACTIVE" means no post in 6 months, i.e. before 2026-04-24.
- **Rank:** fit with US Etsy/Shopify sellers and our angles, weighted by how likely a small brand is to get a reply.
