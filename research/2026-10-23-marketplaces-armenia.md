# Marketplaces that can pay us in Armenia: Ticket #13

**Author:** Brand & Market Researcher · **Date checked:** 2026-10-23 (every check below was made on this date unless noted)
**Question:** Besides Gumroad, which sales channels (a) let an individual living in Armenia sign up and get paid to an Armenian bank account, or by another method that works in Armenia, and (b) bring their own buyers for spreadsheet templates aimed at Etsy and Shopify sellers?

Legend: **V** = verified on the official page today. **U** = unverified (third-party source, search snippet, or the check needs a logged-in account). No accounts were created and no forms were filled in. Every option below is for the owner as an Armenian resident. None involves registering under another country.

## Verdict (summary)

- **Most of the obvious Gumroad alternatives are ruled out by the payment rails, not by the marketplace.** Payhip, Ko-fi, Sellfy and the Notion Marketplace pay sellers only through Stripe or PayPal (or regional gateways that don't cover Armenia). **Stripe doesn't support Armenia** (V), and **PayPal can't pay out to Armenia**: Creative Market's payout table lists PayPal as "Unavailable" for Armenia (V), which matches the 2024 reports (U).
- **The one strong new channel is Creative Market.** It has its own buyers, it already sells Google Sheets budget and bookkeeping templates, and it pays Armenia by **wire, with Creative Market covering the wire fee** (V). The catches: we keep **50%** (V), and the shop needs an approved application.
- **Creative Fabrica** is the runner-up. Spreadsheet templates already sell there, it pays to Payoneer or a bank account, and you can sell the same product elsewhere (V). Armenia support and the commission rate are **unverified**.
- **TemplateMonster** accepts Google Sheets templates and pays by Payoneer or wire, but since 2026-07-01 the author gets only **20%** of their own price (V). It only makes sense as an extra listing that takes little effort.
- **Lemon Squeezy** lists Armenia for bank payouts (V) and costs less than Gumroad, but it is checkout only and brings no buyers. Keep it as a fallback checkout, not a traffic channel.
- **Payoneer** can work as a payout bridge: Armenia appears in Payoneer's own fee tables (V). Withdrawing to an Armenian bank is plausible, but Payoneer publishes no Armenia-specific confirmation (U). **Wise doesn't work**: Armenia isn't on Wise's list of countries where residents can hold money, so we can't receive payouts there (V).
- **Gumroad Discover** costs nothing to set up and is worth switching on now (category, tags, ratings), but it only starts once the account reaches **$100 in genuine sales** and passes a risk review (about 3 weeks) (V). Don't expect much from it: all 9 seller-tool products we checked on Gumroad on 2026-10-21 had 0 ratings.

## 2. Payoneer and Wise as payout bridges

### Payoneer: usable in principle; check withdrawal to an Armenian bank at sign-up
- **Armenia is served.** Payoneer's own fee table for receiving accounts has an Armenia row (1.00% on USD payments, minimum 1.00 USD): https://payoneer.custhelp.com/app/answers/detail/a_id/38762/ (V). Payoneer publishes no full country list; eligibility is shown during sign-up (U).
- **Withdrawal to an Armenian bank:** Payoneer says it withdraws "to your local bank in 190+ countries and territories" (https://www.payoneer.com/withdraw-funds/), but no official page names Armenia or the AMD (U). A law-firm blog (2025-12-09) says Converse Bank has added Payoneer to its mobile app; neither bank's own page confirms this (U): https://armenian-lawyer.com/immigration/using-payoneer-for-an-armenian-llc-and-the-benefits-of-the-payoneer-converse-bank-partnership/
- **Costs** (https://www.payoneer.com/pricing/, V): marketplace payouts in are "varies by marketplace"; bank withdrawal is 1.2–4% (some countries have a minimum fee of up to $20); the **annual fee is $29.95 if the account receives less than $6,000 in 12 months**, which is likely at our volume.
- **Who pays out to Payoneer:** Creative Fabrica (V), TemplateMonster (V), Envato through its bank-transfer option (U). **Gumroad doesn't** (V). Creative Market isn't a Payoneer partner; its guide mentions ACH to a Payoneer US receiving account, but ACH shows as unavailable for Armenia, and Creative Market's free wire makes Payoneer unnecessary there.
- **Verdict:** only open a Payoneer account if we join Creative Fabrica or TemplateMonster. Before relying on it, the owner should confirm in the sign-up flow that USD can be withdrawn to an Armenian bank (ideally Ameriabank), and what it costs.

### Wise: not usable for receiving
- Wise's list of "where you need to live to hold money with Wise" has 80 countries and territories. **Armenia isn't on it** (Georgia is): https://wise.com/help/articles/2813542/where-do-i-need-to-live-to-hold-money-with-wise (V). Without a balance there are no account details, so marketplaces can't pay us through Wise. Armenia isn't on Wise's "unsupported countries" list, so the owner may still be able to **send** money (https://wise.com/help/articles/2978049/which-countries-can-i-use-wise-in, V), and foreign senders can pay **into** Armenian bank accounts with Wise. Neither helps us collect marketplace payouts.

## 3. Gumroad Discover: worth switching on, with low expectations

**Rules** (https://gumroad.com/help/article/79-gumroad-discover, V):
- The **account** must have payout settings filled in, reach a **$100 balance from genuine sales** (no self-purchases or friends), and pass a **risk review that takes about 3 weeks on average** after the $100 mark.
- The **product** must have at least **one successful sale** (refunds don't count), a **category** selected, and **ratings enabled**.
- Products on Discover are **automatically opted into Gumroad Affiliates**.

**Fees:** a Discover sale costs **30% flat**, which includes processing. That leaves $8.40 on a $12 sale, compared with about $9.65 on a direct sale (V). The 30% applies only to buyers who come through Discover, so traffic we bring ourselves still costs 10% + $0.50.

**What helps a product appear:** Gumroad documents only **category, detailed and specific tags, and enabled ratings** (V). Gumroad doesn't document its ranking. It is likely, but unverified, that ratings and recent sales matter, which is why the free lite product should collect ratings.

**Assessment (recommendation):** worth doing because it costs nothing: set the category, write tags such as "etsy seller spreadsheet", "profit tracker", "shopify fees" and "google sheets", and make sure ratings are on. The $100 threshold is about 9 tracker sales, so Discover can't help with the first sales. Our own traffic (Pinterest, Reddit) has to get us there. Evidence of demand is weak: all 9 seller-tool products we checked on Gumroad on 2026-10-21 had 0 ratings ([previous report](2026-10-21-market-and-names.md)).

## Open questions for the owner

1. **Do you want to apply to Creative Market?** It needs an online portfolio page that shows the MarginKite account email (for example a free Behance or Dribbble page, or our Gumroad profile with the email shown), plus ID and a W-8BEN tax form. Is that acceptable?
2. **Incoming SWIFT fees at Ameriabank:** what does Ameriabank charge for an incoming USD wire? It decides whether monthly Creative Market wires of about $20–$60 are worth it. Ask Ameriabank, or check your tariff.
3. **US tax withholding:** Creative Market (through Tipalti) and others collect a W-8BEN and may withhold US tax on royalties. Whether Armenia has a treaty rate is **unverified**. Please ask your accountant along with the Gumroad income question.
4. **PayPal check (optional, about 1 minute):** if you already have a PayPal account, does it show "receive payments" as available for Armenia? If yes, Payhip, Ko-fi and Sellfy reopen (still with little buyer traffic).
5. **Payoneer:** only if we choose Creative Fabrica or TemplateMonster. Are you willing to open a Payoneer account? Withdrawal to an Armenian bank should be confirmed during sign-up, and the $29.95 annual fee applies below $6,000 a year.
6. **Licensing on Creative Market:** list at $12 under the Personal license ($9 minimum), or at $14+ under Commercial? Buyers will use the tracker in their own business, so we should confirm which license tier fits before listing.
7. **Gumroad Discover:** OK to set categories and tags and enable ratings on both products now (no cost, no new listing)?
