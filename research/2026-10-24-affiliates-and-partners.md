# Ticket #20: Gumroad affiliates and an Etsy/Shopify partner shortlist

Brand & Market Researcher, 2026-10-24. Everything below was checked on 2026-10-24 unless another date is given. Recommendations are labelled **Recommendation**; everything else is evidence with a source.

**Verdict:** Gumroad's built-in affiliate tool is enough for us: no extra software and no extra fee beyond the normal sale fee, which is split with the affiliate. At $12 the commission is small money for a creator, so I recommend **40%**, not 30%. The most reachable partners are small Etsy podcasters and YouTubers (4k–50k) who already run affiliate links for Etsy tools: Lizzie Smiley, Sarah Waggoner, Simply POD with Mandy, Gold City Ventures and HeatherXStudio.

## Part A: the Gumroad affiliate program

Sources (Gumroad Help Center, read 2026-10-24):
- [G1] Affiliates on Gumroad: https://gumroad.com/help/article/333-affiliates-on-gumroad
- [G2] Becoming an affiliate on Gumroad (affiliate FAQ): https://gumroad.com/help/article/249-affiliate-faq
- [G3] Gumroad's fees: https://gumroad.com/help/article/66-gumroads-fees
- [G4] Getting paid by Gumroad: https://gumroad.com/help/article/13-getting-paid
- [G5] Gumroad Discover: https://gumroad.com/help/article/79-gumroad-discover

### A1. Two programs

| | **Your Affiliates** (direct) | **Gumroad Affiliates** (formerly "Global Affiliates") |
|---|---|---|
| Who | People we add or approve [G1] | Anyone; they make their own links from Gumroad's dashboard, no need to ask us [G1][G2] |
| Commission | We choose 1%–75% [G1] | Fixed 10% [G1] |
| Cookie window | 30 days [G1][G2] | 7 days [G1][G2] |
| Applies to | Products we pick, or "All products" [G1] | Products on Gumroad Discover. Discover products are opted in automatically [G5] |
| Opt out | Remove the affiliate any time [G1] | Toggle "Opt out of the Gumroad Affiliate Program" on the Affiliates dashboard. It applies to all products and does not affect our own affiliates [G1] |

Notes:
- Discover listing needs a $100 balance from genuine sales, a risk review (about 3 weeks after passing $100), a category, ratings on, and at least one sale per product [G5]. So we are probably not in Gumroad Affiliates yet, but we will be once we qualify, unless the owner opts out.
- Gumroad does not say whether a Gumroad Affiliates sale is charged the 10% direct fee or the 30% Discover fee. This is an open question (see the end).

### A2. How a seller adds affiliates

**Method 1, manual** [G1]:
- Affiliates Dashboard (sidebar, under Analytics) > Add affiliate (https://gumroad.com/affiliates/new).
- Enter the email of the affiliate's Gumroad account. The affiliate must already have a Gumroad account with a confirmed email (needed for payouts).
- Pick one or more products, or "All products".
- Set the commission percentage.
- Optionally set a Destination URL.
- Gumroad emails the affiliate a unique link like `gumroad.com/a/1234567`. If an affiliate has several products, the main link goes to our profile page, and they also get one link per product.
- We can edit the rate or products any time (pencil icon). Edits don't change the link [G1].
- The same email can't be added twice; edit the existing record instead. A product can't have a collaborator and an affiliate at the same time [G1].

**Commission base** [G1]: what the customer pays after any discount, including any tip, excluding sales tax, VAT and shipping. The affiliate earns that percentage "less their share of the fees".

**Attribution** [G1]:
- Last click wins: the most recently clicked affiliate link gets the sale.
- No credit if the browser blocks cookies, unless the affiliate uses a "sticky" link (`.../l/<product>?affiliate_id=<id>`) [G2].
- No credit if the sale is refunded.

**Discount codes** [G1]: an offer code can be attached to an affiliate link (`gumroad.com/a/<id>?offer_code=CODE`). This does not work if the link redirects or goes to the profile page. Alternative: `marginkite.gumroad.com/l/<product>/<code>?affiliate_id=<id>`.

**Messaging affiliates** [G1]: Posts > audience "Affiliates only". **Tracking**: Affiliates dashboard (sales per affiliate), Payouts dashboard (commission subtracted), and the customer CSV has an affiliate column [G1].

### A3. Public "become an affiliate" signup page: yes

**Method 2, "Let affiliates sign up on their own"** [G1]:
- Open the Affiliate Signup Form: https://gumroad.com/affiliate_requests/onboarding_form (owner must be logged in).
- The "Affiliate link" section gives a public link where anyone can request to become our affiliate. Applicants describe their sales strategy.
- The "Affiliate products" section auto-assigns chosen published products at a base commission to everyone we approve.
- We approve or ignore each request on the affiliate dashboard.
- To enable it: the owner sets the products and base rate on that form, then shares the link, for example in the Gumroad bio or a "Partners" line on the product page. Publishing that link counts as a public change, so it needs owner approval.

### A4. How and when affiliates are paid

- Affiliates are paid the same way, and on the same weekly schedule, as creators [G1][G2].
- **Hold:** after 7 days the affiliate can be paid out for a sale [G1]. On weekly, monthly and quarterly schedules every sale has a minimum 7-day hold [G4].
- **Minimum:** payouts need a $100 balance, or more in some local currencies [G4]. An affiliate who has been paid and is then hit by a refund can go negative and must earn back over $100 [G2].
- **Refunds:** the customer gets 100% back and the affiliate loses their commission [G1][G2]. Gumroad returns its own fee on the refunded part, but keeps the payment-processing part [G3].
- **Memberships:** commission recurs on renewals [G1]. Not relevant now; our products are one-time.

**Why the $100 minimum matters for us:** a small creator earning about $3–5 per tracker sale needs 20–35 sales before they see any money. That argues for a higher rate (see A6).

### A5. Fees on an affiliate sale, and a worked example

Gumroad fees [G3]:
- Direct sales: 10% + $0.50 per transaction, plus card processing of 2.9% + $0.30. PayPal fees differ.
- Discover sales: a flat 30%, all-in.
- Affiliate sales: "fees on affiliate purchases are split between the affiliate and the creator proportional to the affiliate's commission". At 30% commission the affiliate pays 30% of the fees [G1][G3].
- There is no separate affiliate-program fee.

**Worked example: $12 tracker, our own affiliate at 30%**

Assumptions:
- (a1) The buyer pays by card through Gumroad's normal checkout. A PayPal payment has different processing fees.
- (a2) $12 is the price before tax. Sales tax is collected on top and does not change our share; the commission base excludes tax [G1].
- (a3) No discount code and no tip.
- (a4) It is not a Discover sale.
- (a5) We are under $20,000 in sales for the month, so the full 10% fee applies [G3].

| Step | Amount |
|---|---|
| Sale price | $12.00 |
| Gumroad fee 10% + $0.50 | $1.70 |
| Processing 2.9% + $0.30 | $0.348 + $0.30 = $0.648 |
| **Total fees** | **$2.348** |
| Affiliate gross commission, 30% × $12 | $3.60 |
| Affiliate's share of fees, 30% × $2.348 | −$0.704 |
| **Affiliate receives** | **$2.90** |
| Our gross share, 70% × $12 | $8.40 |
| Our share of fees, 70% × $2.348 | −$1.644 |
| **We net** | **$6.76** |
| Check: $2.90 + $6.76 + $2.35 = $12.00 | ✓ |

Shortcut: net after fees is $12 − $2.348 = $9.652, the "$9.65 we keep" in the log. With an affiliate at rate r, we net $9.652 × (1 − r) and the affiliate gets $9.652 × r.

| Commission | Affiliate gets | We net | Affiliate sales to reach the $100 payout |
|---|---|---|---|
| none (direct sale) | – | $9.65 | – |
| 10% (Gumroad Affiliates, if the direct fee applies; not confirmed) | $0.97 | $8.69 | 104 |
| 10% (Gumroad Affiliates, if the 30% Discover fee applies) | about $0.84 | about $7.56 | about 119 |
| 30% | $2.90 | $6.76 | 35 |
| **40%** | **$3.86** | **$5.79** | **26** |
| 50% | $4.83 | $4.83 | 21 |

- The 10% rows assume the 10% is taken on the same fee-split basis. Gumroad does not document this, so those rows are marked as assumptions.
- **Free lite calculator:** it costs $0, so there is no commission. Assigning an affiliate "All products" should still credit them if someone clicks their lite link and buys the tracker within 30 days in the same browser. This is inferred from the 30-day cookie rule in [G1], not stated outright. Test it before promising it.

### A6. Recommendation: 40% on the tracker for our own affiliates

**Recommendation**, not evidence:
- **40%, assigned on "All products".** The affiliate earns $3.86 per sale and we keep $5.79.
- **Why not 30%:** $2.90 per sale is hard to pitch. A creator needs 35 sales before Gumroad pays out anything (the $100 minimum [G4]). The Etsy tools these creators already promote pay recurring commissions, which beat a one-time $2.90: eRank pays 30% recurring for 12 months, Alura 30% recurring for 6 months (per the slice-3 checks of erank.com and alura.io/affiliate).
- **Why not 50%:** it halves our take to $4.83. Fees are split proportionally, so every affiliate sale still makes money (a digital file costs nothing to deliver). But we would have nothing left for a launch discount through the affiliate link. 50% is better kept for one standout partner if needed. Gumroad lets us set the rate per affiliate [G1].
- **Product pages:** use the 30-day cookie and **sticky links** for creators whose audience may block cookies.
- **Gumroad Affiliates (10%):** stay opted in. It costs nothing until it brings a sale. Revisit if the 30% Discover fee turns out to stack.
- **Honest pricing:** affiliate discount codes must be real, time-limited promotions, never an inflated price (project rule). Ask affiliates to disclose the link as an affiliate link (FTC endorsement rules; that is the creator's responsibility, but we should say it in the invite).

## Open questions for the owner

1. **Commission:** approve 40% on "All products" for our own affiliates? Or 30% or 50%?
2. **Gumroad Affiliates (10%, automatic once we reach Discover):** stay in or opt out? I recommend staying in. Could you ask Gumroad support whether those sales pay the 30% Discover fee or the 10% + $0.50 direct fee? The help pages don't say.
3. **Signup form:** enable the public affiliate signup form (https://gumroad.com/affiliate_requests/onboarding_form, needs your login) and add its link to the Gumroad bio? That is a public change, so it needs your approval.
4. **Launch code:** offer affiliates a real, time-limited discount code for their audience (for example 20% off for 14 days)? This follows the honest-pricing rule. At 40% commission on a $9.60 sale we would net about $4.54: fees are $0.96 + $0.50 + $0.28 + $0.30 = $2.04, so $9.60 − $2.04 = $7.56, and 60% of that is $4.54 (assumption: same fee formula).
5. **Outreach:** approve the outreach wording before any message goes out? Who sends it, and from marginkite.shop@gmail.com? Five of the top 10 need you to reveal the YouTube email by hand (CAPTCHA), or need a contact-form submission.
6. **Lite-to-tracker credit:** test whether a lite click credits the affiliate when the tracker is bought later. It would take one $12 test purchase plus a refund; the refund returns Gumroad's fee but not processing [G3]. Is that worth roughly $0.65?
7. **Paid placement:** consider Craft Industry Alliance's $275 newsletter slot later? This is a spend decision; not now.
