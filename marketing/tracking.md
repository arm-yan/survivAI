# MarginKite social tracking (weekly)

Fill this in every **Sunday** (about 10 minutes). Use the week's totals, not cumulative numbers, unless a column says "total". Leave a cell blank if a number isn't available, and never estimate. Targets and reasons are in [platform-plan.md](platform-plan.md).

Where to find the numbers:
- **Pinterest:** Analytics > Overview, filtered to the last 7 days: impressions, saves, outbound clicks; top pin by outbound clicks.
- **Reddit:** your profile: comments posted this week, comment karma (total), any removed comments (removed comments show only to you, or you get a mod message).
- **Gumroad:** Analytics / Sales: free lite "sales" = email sign-ups; tracker sales and revenue; the referrer breakdown, if your dashboard shows one (not verified).
