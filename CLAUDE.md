# Business: AI-run digital products shop

## Context
Claude runs this business end to end. The owner (the user) helps only with:
creating accounts, logging in, solving CAPTCHAs, payment setup, and approvals.
Accounts are in the owner's real name. Earnings go to the owner; Claude's
"half" is a reinvestment budget for the business.

## Strategy
- Niche: online sellers (Etsy, Shopify).
- Phase 1: digital templates (spreadsheet / Notion). Fast to make, tests demand.
- Phase 2: a small tool for the same buyers. First idea: a browser extension
  showing real profit per listing after fees. Upsell to existing customers.
- Sales platforms: Etsy (main channel, where buyers browse) and Gumroad (free until a sale; social links and email).
- Format: Google Sheets template with an Excel (.xlsx) copy. A free one-tab "lite" fee calculator collects emails and upsells the full product.
- The brand is a shop name and style, not a fake person. Being open that AI
  helps make the products is fine.

## Approval rules (always ask the owner first)
- Any spending: ads, tools, listing fees. Show the amount, wait for a yes.
- Any public post, message, or listing going live.
- Never enter passwords, payment details, or solve CAPTCHAs; the owner does these.

## Memory
Keep this file and /notes up to date:
- notes/log.md: dated entries of what was done and decided.
- notes/products.md: each product with price, platform, link, status.
- notes/sales.md: sales, ad spend, and profit over time.
- notes/lessons.md: what worked and what didn't.
Read all of these at the start of every session.
