# Lessons

Add what worked and what didn't, with dates.

## 2026-10-24/25
- **Check platform country support before planning a channel.** Etsy (our planned main channel) doesn't allow shops from Armenia. Most PayPal- or Stripe-based marketplaces can't pay out to Armenia either. Gumroad (SWIFT to a personal Ameria account) works.
- **Creative Market rejected the shop permanently** ("does not meet our guidelines or community standards"; future applications won't be reviewed). Likely reasons: no spreadsheet category (we filed under Print > Stationery), a thin portfolio (2 products), and AI disclosed as primary creation. Before applying to curated marketplaces, check that the product category exists and that AI-made shops are welcome.
- **Some newsletters are paid-only.** Ecommerce Coffee Break doesn't do affiliate or rev-share deals. Media businesses with public rate cards rarely take affiliate offers; target creators instead.
- **Send outreach with Gmail's full-page compose** (/mail/u/1/?view=cm&fs=1). The pop-up compose minimizes when the browser window resizes and loses text.
- **Verify contact addresses before sending.** Two research-supplied addresses failed (gretecreates.com doesn't exist; Tin Teddy bounced twice). One contact page asked for no unsolicited products, and we respected it.
