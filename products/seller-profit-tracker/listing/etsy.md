## Description (paste as is)

```
See what you really keep from each Etsy or Shopify sale after fees, product cost and shipping. Google Sheets + Excel. US sellers. Fees dated 2026-10-21. Digital download: nothing is shipped.

WHO IT'S FOR
US Etsy sellers who want to know their real profit per order, and anyone wondering whether a Shopify store would leave them more.

THE PROBLEM
Etsy takes a listing fee, a transaction fee on item plus shipping, payment processing and, on some orders, an Offsite Ads fee. Refunds give some of it back, and some of it they don't. It's easy to price an item and find out later that the fees ate most of the margin.

WHAT IT DOES
- Works out every Etsy fee for each order: listing, transaction, payment processing and Offsite Ads (15% or 12%, with the $100 cap)
- Puts Etsy and Shopify side by side for one item: every fee line, net profit and margin for both
- Reverse mode: type a target margin (say 30%) and it gives the item price to charge on Etsy and on Shopify
- "When does Shopify pay off?": the number of orders a month at which a Shopify plan pays for itself, at your price
- Orders Log with room for 500 orders: discounts, refunds, partial refunds and Etsy fee credits on refunds
- Products tab turns materials, packaging and labour minutes into a unit cost
- Dashboard: 12 months of orders, revenue, fees, costs, overhead and net profit, your top 3 and bottom 3 products, and 2 charts (net profit by month, where your fees went)

WHAT'S INCLUDED (7 tabs)
1. Start Here: setup steps, colour key, sample-data and BLANK versions explained
2. Settings: dated fee table with source and status for every rate; Shopify plan, billing and payment provider pickers
3. Products: unit cost from materials, packaging and labour
4. Price Calculator: Etsy vs Shopify, target-margin price, Shopify break-even
5. Orders Log: one row per order, 500 rows ready
6. Expenses: monthly overhead, Etsy Ads spend, Shopify plan bill
7. Dashboard: monthly profit, top and bottom products, 2 charts

HOW IT WORKS
1. Make your copy in Google Sheets (two links in the included PDF), or open an .xlsx in Excel.
2. Settings: your hourly rate, your Etsy sales level, your Shopify plan.
3. Products: add what you sell. Unit cost is worked out for you.
4. Orders Log: one row per order. Fees and profit fill in.
5. Dashboard: read your monthly profit and margin.
You get two versions: one with sample data so you can see it working first, and a BLANK one to start fresh.

FORMAT AND COMPATIBILITY

- You get 3 files: the workbook with sample data (.xlsx), the same workbook BLANK (.xlsx), and a 1-page PDF guide with two Google Sheets "make a copy" links (sample data and BLANK).
- Works in Google Sheets (free Google account) and Excel 2016 or newer, Windows or Mac.
- No macros, no add-ons, no external links. Sheets are protected with no password, so you can unlock and change anything.
- Yellow cells are for you to type in. Blue cells are calculated and locked so you don't overwrite them by accident.

PLEASE NOTE
```

## FAQ (paste into the description or answer in messages; Etsy has no FAQ field)

**1. Is anything shipped?**
No. This is a digital download. After you buy, Etsy gives you 3 files: the workbook with sample data, the same workbook BLANK for your own shop, and a 1-page PDF guide.

**2. Do I need Excel?**
No. The included PDF has two Google Sheets links (sample data and BLANK) that make your own copy with a free Google account. Or open either .xlsx in Excel 2016 or newer, Windows or Mac.

**3. Does it work outside the US?**
Not yet. This version uses US Etsy and Shopify fees. You can edit the rates in Settings, but other countries' rules (like Etsy's Regulatory Operating Fee) aren't built in.

**4. What happens when Etsy changes its fees?**
Every rate lives in one table on the Settings tab with its date and source. Change the value there and every order, calculator and chart updates.

**5. Can it import my Etsy orders?**
Not in this version. You type one row per order in the Orders Log, which has 500 rows ready.

**6. I don't sell on Shopify. Is it still useful?**
Yes. Fees, profit, refunds, the Dashboard and reverse pricing all work with Etsy orders only. The Shopify column just shows what the same sale would look like there.

**7. Are the numbers exact?**
They are estimates based on the fee rates in Settings. Always check them against your own Etsy and Shopify statements. It isn't tax or accounting advice.

**8. Was this made with AI?**
Yes, partly. AI helped design and build the template, and every formula was checked against hand-worked examples before release.
