# MarginKite Seller Profit Tracker: Quick Start

Thanks for buying, and welcome. This page gets you to your first real profit number in about 15 minutes.

## 1. Make your Google Sheets copy

1. Sign in to your Google account.
2. Open this link: **[[PLACEHOLDER: Google Sheets "make a copy" link]]**
3. Click **Make a copy**. The copy is yours, in your own Google Drive.

No link handy? Upload the .xlsx to Google Drive, open it, then choose **File > Save as Google Sheets**.

## 2. Or open it in Excel

Open `MarginKite-Seller-Profit-Tracker.xlsx` in **Excel 2016 or newer** (Windows or Mac). If you see **Enable Editing**, click it. There are no macros to allow.

## 3. Five steps to your first result

1. **Settings:** enter your hourly rate, answer the Etsy $10k question, and pick your Shopify plan, billing and payment provider.
2. **Products:** add each product with materials, packaging and labour minutes. Unit cost is worked out for you.
3. **Price Calculator:** pick a product and enter price, shipping charged and your shipping cost. Read Etsy vs Shopify profit, the price for your target margin, and when Shopify pays off.
4. **Orders Log:** add one row per order (yellow columns A–L). Fees and profit fill in.
5. **Expenses, then Dashboard:** add monthly overhead, set the first month on the Dashboard, and read your monthly profit.

**Yellow** cells are for typing. **Blue** cells are calculated and locked (no password). The file starts with sample data; the **Start Here** tab lists the cells to clear.

## 4. Fees and disclaimer

- Fees are **US Etsy and Shopify fees as of 2026-10-21**. Each rate shows its date, source and status on the **Settings** tab. When a fee changes, edit it there and the whole workbook updates.
- Rows marked **UNVERIFIED** or **ASSUMPTION** are best estimates. Check your Shopify card rate in your Shopify admin.
- All figures are **estimates**. Check them against your own Etsy and Shopify statements. This is not tax or accounting advice.
- US sellers only in this version.
