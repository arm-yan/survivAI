# MarginKite Seller Profit Tracker: Quick Start

Thanks for buying. Everything is in this download: two Excel workbooks and this guide. This page gets you to your first real profit number in about 15 minutes.

## 1. Open it in Excel

Open `MarginKite-Seller-Profit-Tracker.xlsx` (sample data) or `MarginKite-Seller-Profit-Tracker-BLANK.xlsx` (empty) in **Excel 2016 or newer**, Windows or Mac. Click **Enable Editing** if asked. There are no macros.

## 2. Or use Google Sheets

Upload an .xlsx to Google Drive and choose **File > Save as Google Sheets**. Shortcut: sign in to Google, open a link below, and click **Make a copy**. The copy is yours, in your own Google Drive.

- **With sample data:** [Make a copy: tracker with sample data](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1EC3WQBv85J-L67KSHyWd0U1QExs7YLxZFE5qH7lfojw/copy)
- **BLANK:** [Make a copy: BLANK tracker](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1I5_ppRbvAYHL0KJ4ljvBSgQvCpiCP-S8ohdihBVczeo/copy)

**Which one?** Use the sample-data version to see how it works, and the BLANK version for your own shop.

## 3. Five steps to your first result

1. **Settings:** enter your hourly rate, answer the Etsy $10k question, and pick your Shopify plan, billing and payment provider.
2. **Products:** add each product with materials, packaging and labour minutes. Unit cost is worked out for you.
3. **Price Calculator:** pick a product, enter price, shipping charged and shipping cost. Read Etsy vs Shopify profit, the price for your target margin, and when Shopify pays off.
4. **Orders Log:** one row per order (yellow columns A–L). Fees and profit fill in.
5. **Expenses, then Dashboard:** add monthly overhead, set the first month, and read your monthly profit.

**Yellow** = you type. **Blue** = calculated, locked with no password. The sample-data version has 5 products, 30 orders and 3 months of expenses (Start Here lists the cells to clear). The BLANK version starts empty with the same fee settings.

## 4. Fees and disclaimer

- **US Etsy and Shopify fees as of 2026-10-21.** Each rate shows its date, source and status on **Settings**; edit a rate there and the whole workbook updates. Rows marked UNVERIFIED or ASSUMPTION are best estimates; check your Shopify card rate in your Shopify admin.
- All figures are **estimates**. Check your own Etsy and Shopify statements. Not tax or accounting advice. US sellers only.
