## Description (paste as is)

```
See what you really keep from each Etsy or Shopify sale after fees, product cost and shipping.

WHO IT'S FOR
US Etsy sellers who want their real profit per order, and anyone asking "would Shopify leave me more?"

THE PROBLEM
Etsy charges a listing fee, a transaction fee on item plus shipping, payment processing and sometimes an Offsite Ads fee. Refunds give part of it back. Shopify swaps most of that for a monthly plan and a card fee. Comparing the two on your own numbers takes an afternoon with a calculator.

WHAT IT DOES
✔ Every Etsy fee for each order: listing, transaction, payment processing and Offsite Ads (15% or 12%, with the $100 cap)
✔ Etsy vs Shopify side by side for one item: every fee line, net profit and margin
✔ Reverse mode: type a target margin and get the item price to charge on Etsy and on Shopify
✔ "When does Shopify pay off?": the orders a month at which a Shopify plan pays for itself, at your price
✔ Orders Log for 500 orders, with discounts, refunds, partial refunds and Etsy fee credits
✔ Unit cost from materials, packaging and labour minutes
✔ Dashboard: 12 months of revenue, fees, costs, overhead and net profit; top 3 and bottom 3 products; 2 charts

WHAT'S INCLUDED: 7 TABS
Start Here · Settings (dated fee table) · Products · Price Calculator · Orders Log · Expenses · Dashboard

HOW IT WORKS
1. Make your Google Sheets copy (two links in the PDF) or open an .xlsx in Excel.
2. Settings: hourly rate, Etsy sales level, Shopify plan.
3. Products: add what you sell.
4. Orders Log: one row per order. Fees and profit fill in.
5. Dashboard: read your monthly profit.
You get two versions: one with sample data to see how it works, and a BLANK one for your own shop.
```

## FAQ (Gumroad has no FAQ field: paste under the description or use as canned answers)

**1. Is anything shipped?**
No. It's a digital download, available right after checkout and in your Gumroad library: the workbook with sample data, the same workbook BLANK for your own shop, and a 1-page PDF guide.

**2. Do I need Excel?**
No. The PDF has two links (sample data and BLANK) that make your own Google Sheets copy with a free Google account. Or open either .xlsx in Excel 2016 or newer.

**3. Is it for sellers outside the US?**
Not yet. It uses US Etsy and Shopify fees. You can edit any rate in Settings, but other countries' fee rules aren't built in.

**4. What if fees change?**
Every rate sits in one dated table on Settings. Change the value and the whole workbook updates.

**5. Does it import my orders?**
No. You type one row per order. There are 500 rows ready.

**6. I only sell on Etsy. Is it still worth it?**
Yes. Fees, refunds, profit, reverse pricing and the Dashboard all work with Etsy orders alone.

**7. How accurate is it?**
It gives estimates from the rates in Settings. Check against your own Etsy and Shopify statements. It isn't tax or accounting advice.

**8. Did AI make this?**
AI helped design and build it. Every formula was checked against hand-worked examples before release.
